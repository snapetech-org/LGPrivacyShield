# PrivacyShield app guide

See the [README screenshots](../README.md#screenshots) for every page in dark and light themes. They use sample C5/webOS 25 data.

## Overview

Shows your TV software, Live Plus and voice status. **Review controls** opens the settings page.

## Controls

PrivacyShield shows settings available on the connected TV. A switch is enabled after its current value can be read, and each change is checked on the TV.

Use **Apply recommended settings** or change switches individually. **Save current settings** saves readable values as a reusable restore point. If no point exists, the first change saves the starting values. **Restore saved settings** reapplies the saved values; a manual save remains available afterward.

The position indicator shows where you are in the list. Use Up and Down on the remote to move through controls.

## LG agreements

Review choices saved on the TV and their documented effects. Use **Open LG User Agreements** to change a choice in LG's screen.

## About

Shows the app version, license, source code, and a Ko-fi support QR code and link: [ko-fi.com/snapetech](https://ko-fi.com/snapetech).

## Remote and theme

Use arrows to move and OK to select. Back returns to Overview, then exits the app. PrivacyShield starts in dark theme; **Use light theme** switches palettes and saves your choice on the TV.

## Compatibility report

When your TV is unmapped or a setting cannot be read, choose **Prepare compatibility report** in Controls. Scan its single QR code with a phone to open GitHub's prefilled form; the phone sends the report in the link, and submitting creates a public issue. It contains model and software details, service access, and yes/no control-read results; it excludes setting values and agreement choices.

For installation, see the [TV guide](TEST-ON-YOUR-TV.md). For evidence and setting effects, see the [documentation index](README.md).
