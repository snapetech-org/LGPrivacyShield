Consent and services

LG agreements

Review the saved choices and their documented effects. Change them in LG User Agreements.

Agreement names and terms can vary by region, model, and software. These notes summarize LG's published descriptions; read the exact text shown on this TV.

An accepted agreement can make a feature eligible; it does not turn the feature on. Status shows the saved choice, not whether data was used or sent.

ACR also requires Live Plus to be on. Voice recognition requires a voice action or an enabled hands-free feature. These controls are separate from consent.

Viewing Information Agreement

Optional

Accepted: Live Plus can use ACR when it is on. LG says ACR may process content fingerprints with device, source, and network information for audience analysis; some source-aware voice commands may also need this agreement.

Not accepted: ACR is unavailable; some source-aware voice commands may be limited.
