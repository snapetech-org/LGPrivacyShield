Viewing Information Agreement

Optional

Accepted: Live Plus can use ACR when it is on. LG says ACR may process content fingerprints with device, source, and network information for audience analysis; some source-aware voice commands may also need this agreement.

Not accepted: ACR is unavailable; some source-aware voice commands may be limited.

Voice Information

Optional

Accepted: LG voice recognition can run after a voice action, such as pressing the remote microphone button or enabling supported hands-free voice. Consent alone does not activate it.

Not accepted: LG voice recognition is unavailable. Voice wake and hands-free settings remain separate controls.

Optional
